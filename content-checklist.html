Worth considering

Two pages you might want later

These come from reading your own content rather than from a study of other practices. We have not benchmarked your site against other therapists in Cambridge, and we can do that separately if it would be useful.

A fees or getting started pagePeople very often look for this before making contact. It does not have to name prices; it can simply explain how an initial session works and what happens next.
Frequently asked questionsA short set of questions, for example how long sessions last, how many people usually come for, and what happens in a first session, tends to reduce the hesitation before someone gets in touch.
