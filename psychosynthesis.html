Psychosynthesis

Psychotherapy through Psychosynthesis

A compassionate framework for revitalising your spirit of Self, for your soul's journey of self-exploration, healing and transformation.

What it is

Growth and wounding, held together

Psychosynthesis is a holistic and integrative approach to psychotherapy that recognises emotional pain and wounding alongside our innate capacity for growth and reaching towards potential.

It offers a way of rediscovering your inner resources and integrating them, so that you can realign with who you truly are. Its founder, the Italian psychiatrist Roberto Assagioli, advocated synthesising the psyche rather than simply analysing it. He was a contemporary of Freud and Jung, working in the fields of transpersonal and humanistic psychology.

The aim of psychosynthesis is to harmonise all aspects of the human being, physical, emotional, mental and spiritual, around a unifying centre.

Roberto Assagioli
The two movements

A psychology of love and will

Assagioli sometimes described Psychosynthesis this way. Between them, these two capacities are what make change possible.

Love

Love offers the compassionate acceptance through which healing becomes possible.

Will

Will is the capacity to choose, to respond consciously and to shape your life with increasing freedom and responsibility.

My own path

Why I chose to train in Psychosynthesis

There's a crack in everything, that's how the light gets in.

Leonard Cohen

My decision grew out of a deeply personal journey. A near-death experience awakened in me a profound appreciation of life and brought a new clarity about my Self and my sense of purpose. Yet it also led me through a series of crises of meaning that challenged the very fabric of my inner world.

I encountered the Psychosynthesis belief that within our deepest wounding lies opportunity, the ground from which growth and transformation emerge. This resonated deeply, so that rather than seeing suffering simply as something to overcome, Psychosynthesis offered a way of meeting it with courage, compassion and curiosity.

I responded to what I came to recognise as the deeper call of Self. As my self-awareness grew, in a considered and caring way, so too did my capacity to exercise my Will for transforming my life.

My Soul's Journey continues to inform my work, giving me a profound trust in each person's innate capacity for healing, growth and transformation, even amid life's greatest challenges.

A poem that inspired me

I will not die an unlived life. I will not live in fear of falling or catching fire. I choose to inhabit my days, to allow my living to open me, to make me less afraid, more accessible, to loosen my heart until it becomes a wing, a torch, a promise. I choose to risk my significance; to live so that which came to me as seed goes to the next as blossom and that which came to me as blossom, goes on as fruit.
