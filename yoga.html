The practice

A quiet companion throughout my adult life

My yoga teaching practice is the Iyengar method. It involves alignment, extension, balance, strength, mobility and focus.

The Sanskrit word yoga means union. Through attentive awareness of the body, and within the body, a spiritual pathway is revealed, offering connection with Self and with humanity.

Yoga does not transform the way we see things, it transforms the person who sees.

B. K. S. Iyengar
Embodied spirituality

Yoga for emotional balance

Everything we do for the body we do for the mind as well.

Emotions are energies in motion

They are experienced in sensations and in the movements of the breath and our breathing body. Mindful emotional tracking calms the scattered thought brain so that things seem more manageable.

In the poses, there is no transaction

In asanas that are passively active, giving time to settle into silence within the body-mind, there is only being. As sensations and energies emerge into consciousness, our capacity for proprioception expands and neural pathways are ignited and strengthened.

Why this practice

What the practice made possible

Your body is the harp of your soul, and it is yours to bring forth sweet music from it or confused sounds.

Kahlil Gibran, in The Prophet

Following a near-death experience at sea, drawing on the inner resilience cultivated through the Iyengar tradition and Buddhist contemplative practices, I managed pain and panic without medication.

That is not a claim about what yoga can do for anyone else. It is simply why I teach the way I do, with attention on the breathing body and on what is actually being felt, rather than on achieving a shape.

A second line I return to

Yoga teaches us to cure what cannot be endured and to endure what cannot be cured.

B. K. S. Iyengar
