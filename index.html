Hello and welcome

Psychotherapy and Counselling in Cambridge & Online

As a transpersonal psychotherapist and counsellor with a foundation in Psychosynthesis, I offer a thoughtful and compassionate space in which healing, understanding and transformation can unfold. I am a UKCP Registered Psychotherapist, working with adults.

‘Creating a still point in a turning world’

Book an initial session

Life can sometimes feel as though it has lost its centre.

You may be living with anxiety or grief, carrying the effects of trauma, struggling in relationships, or simply sensing that something important is asking for your attention. At such times it can be difficult to find solid ground.

Through deeper connection with yourself and others, psychotherapy can offer the opportunity to discover new ways of living with greater freedom, authenticity and fulfilment.

What brings people here

Therapy can help when you are experiencing any of a range of personal difficulties:

  • Addiction, overuse or abuse of substances and compulsive behaviour patterns
  • Anxiety and panic, building emotional resilience and self-trust
  • Complex PTSD
  • Despair, grief, feeling lost and periods of crisis
  • Disordered eating and self-harming behaviour patterns
  • Inflammatory symptoms such as irritable bowel, joint pain, disrupted sleep
  • Loneliness, aloneness, fear of isolation; longing for belonging, boarding school syndrome
  • Maternal ambivalence, regret and confusion
  • Relief from trauma, the inner wounding in your history and relationships that were overwhelming
  • Search for meaning in life and more fulfilment, reconnecting with yourself and others
  • Sex problems and sexual identity
  • The creative spark, imaginative inspiration and self-expression with vitality which may lead to overwhelm

My practice is trauma-informed and sex-positive, offering an inclusive and respectful environment in which every aspect of your experience can be explored safely, at a pace which feels right for you.
