Hello and welcome

A space where you can pause and be heard

I am a UKCP Registered Psychotherapist, working with adults in Cambridge and online. My practice is trauma-informed and sex-positive, offering an inclusive and respectful environment in which every aspect of your experience can be explored safely, at a pace which feels right for you.

Through deeper connection with yourself and others, psychotherapy can offer the opportunity to discover new ways of living with greater freedom, authenticity and fulfilment.

What brings people here

You may recognise something of yourself in this

You may be living with anxiety or grief, carrying the effects of trauma, struggling in relationships, or simply sensing that something important is asking for your attention. At such times it can be difficult to find solid ground.

  • Addiction, overuse or abuse of substances and compulsive behaviour patterns
  • Anxiety and panic, building emotional resilience and self-trust
  • Complex PTSD
  • Despair, grief, feeling lost and periods of crisis
  • Disordered eating and self-harming behaviour patterns
  • Inflammatory symptoms such as irritable bowel, joint pain and disrupted sleep
  • Loneliness, fear of isolation, longing for belonging and boarding school syndrome
  • Maternal ambivalence, regret and confusion
  • Relief from trauma, the inner wounding in your history and relationships that was overwhelming
  • The search for meaning and fulfilment, reconnecting with yourself and others
  • Sex problems and sexual identity
  • The creative spark and self-expression with vitality, which may lead to overwhelm
