Get in touch with Elspeth

Making first contact with a therapist may feel like a huge step, though it is a significant step in choosing to live your life more fully. It is an act of self-compassion.

I offer an initial assessment session for us to discover what is troubling you and to explore how our working together may support the work of transforming your life. You are invited to the calm, safe and confidential space of my consulting room in Cambridge, face to face, or online via Zoom.
